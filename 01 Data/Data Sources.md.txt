Data Sources

The Nigeria Economic Indicators Dashboard uses data from publicly available economic and financial sources.

| Indicator | Source | Frequency | Period |
|---|---|---|---|
| GDP Growth | World Bank | Annual | 2015–2025 |
| Population | World Bank | Annual | 2015–2025 |
| Inflation | IMF | Annual | 2015–2025 |
| Brent Crude Oil Price | FRED | Annual | 2015–2025 |
| Monetary Policy Rate (MPR) | Central Bank of Nigeria | Annual | 2015–2025 |
| Official Exchange Rate | Central Bank of Nigeria | Annual | 2015–2025 |

Notes

The datasets were cleaned and transformed before being imported into Power BI.

Where multiple observations were available within a year, the appropriate aggregation was applied during the data preparation process.

The dashboard is intended for analytical and educational purposes.
